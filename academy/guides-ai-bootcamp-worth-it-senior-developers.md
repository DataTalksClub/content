## Why Beginner Bootcamps Do Not Fit Senior Developers

Most coding bootcamps are built for career changers. They start with programming basics, Git, a web framework and a first deployment, because their students have never done these things.

If you have written production code for years, you already do all of that at work. You would spend most of the weeks on material you could teach yourself, in a group that moves at the speed of people who are new to programming.

A beginner course on a senior CV tells an interviewer little. What they want to hear about is the work you did, and a course only helps if it gives you new work to talk about.

## The Four Questions to Ask About Any Program

Before you give any program three months, check these four things:

1. **Who is in the group?** Ask what experience the program expects. If it says "no prior programming knowledge is required", it is a beginner program, whatever the title says.
2. **What is new for you?** Go through the syllabus week by week and mark what you have already done at work. If most weeks are marked, the program is not for you.
3. **What do you build?** Look for projects you can show and explain in an interview: a product on top of an LLM, or real software shipped with coding agents. Exercises that only follow a tutorial do not count.
4. **Who teaches?** The teacher should do this work, not only teach it. Check what they have built and written.

## When a Short Workshop Is Enough

Short formats of a few hours to a few days help when you need one specific skill, for example setting up a coding agent in your own repository or learning how to evaluate an LLM feature. They work well when you are employed and your team already uses these tools, and you need to catch up on one part of the work.

They do not give you a portfolio project or a new way of working. For that you need weeks of practice, not days.

## When Three Months Full-Time Makes Sense

A full-time program makes sense when you have the time and want to change how you work, not just add one tool:

- you are between jobs and want to use the gap for something you can show
- you are in a notice period and know the end date
- you agreed time for training with your employer

If you are not sure whether full days fit your life, [Full-Time or Part-Time, Berlin or Online](/academy/guides/full-time-or-part-time-ai-bootcamp-germany/) walks through the formats.

Full-time also means full days. Make sure you can be there every day, Monday to Friday, for the whole program before you sign up for anything.

## What Companies Ask Senior Developers About AI

AI skills are now part of ordinary developer job ads, not only of AI roles. In our count of developer jobs in Germany on arbeitnow in the week of 7 to 14 November 2026, 86 of 588 ads named Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex or AI-assisted coding. This is an approximate count we made by searching the ad texts for these terms.

In a sample of 58 AI job ads we collected in November 2026, mostly in Berlin, 35 were for senior, staff or lead roles and 2 were junior. Companies hiring for AI work mostly look for people who can already build and ship software.

## How a Course Looks on a Senior CV

A course on its own does not change how a senior CV reads. What changes it is what you built during the course and what you can say about it.

Treat a program as interview preparation. Write down what you built, which decisions you made, what broke and how you fixed it. In an interview you talk about that work, the same way you talk about your projects at a job. I wrote more about this in the [guide on getting hired as an experienced developer](/academy/guides/getting-hired-with-ai/).

## FAQ

### Is a Coding Bootcamp Worth It for a Senior Developer?

Usually not. Most coding bootcamps teach programming from the start, which a senior developer already does at work.

### What Should a Senior Developer Look for in an AI Program?

A group where everyone already ships code, content on LLM products or coding agents, projects to show in an interview, and a teacher who does this work.

### Can a Senior Developer Learn the Same AI Material for Free?

Yes. The DataTalks.Club Zoomcamps are free and cover the base material. A full-time program adds a fixed schedule, a small group and live teaching.
