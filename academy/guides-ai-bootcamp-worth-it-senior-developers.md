## Why Beginner Bootcamps Do Not Fit Senior Developers

Most coding bootcamps are built for career changers. They start with programming basics, Git, a web framework and a first deployment, because their students have never done any of this.

If you've written production code for years, you already do all of that at work. You'd spend most of the time on things you already know, in a group that moves at the pace of people who are new to programming.

A beginner course on a senior CV doesn't tell an interviewer much. They want to hear about the work you did, so a course only helps if it gives you new work to talk about.

## The Four Questions to Ask About Any Program

Before you give any program three months, check these four things:

1. Who's in the group? Ask what experience you need to get in. If the page says "no prior programming knowledge is required", it's a beginner program, whatever the title says.
2. What's new for you? Go through the syllabus week by week and mark what you've already done at work. If you've marked most of the weeks, the program isn't for you.
3. What do you build? Look for projects you can show and explain in an interview: a product on top of an LLM, or real software shipped with coding agents. Exercises where you just follow a tutorial don't count.
4. Who teaches? The teacher should do this work, not only teach it. Check what they've built and written.

## When a Short Workshop Is Enough

Short formats, from a few hours to a few days, help when you need one specific skill, for example setting up a coding agent in your own repository or learning how to evaluate an LLM feature. They work well if you have a job, your team already uses these tools, and you need to catch up on one part of the work.

You won't get a portfolio project or a new way of working out of them. For that you need weeks of practice, not days.

## When Three Months Full-Time Makes Sense

A full-time program makes sense if you have the time and want to change how you work, not just add one tool:

- you're between jobs and want to spend the gap on something you can show
- you're in a notice period and know the end date
- your employer has agreed to give you time off for training

If you're not sure whether full days fit your life, read [Full-Time or Part-Time, Berlin or Online](/academy/guides/full-time-or-part-time-ai-bootcamp-germany/).

Before you sign up for anything, make sure you can be there every day, Monday to Friday, for the whole program.

## What Companies Ask Senior Developers About AI

Companies now ask for AI skills in ordinary developer job ads, not only in AI roles. We counted developer jobs in Germany on arbeitnow in the week of 7 to 14 November 2026: 86 of 588 ads named Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex or AI-assisted coding. It's an approximate count, since we just searched the ad texts for these terms.

We also collected a sample of 58 AI job ads in November 2026, mostly in Berlin. 35 of them were for senior, staff or lead roles, and 2 were junior. Companies hiring for AI work mostly want people who can already build and ship software.

## How a Course Looks on a Senior CV

A course on its own doesn't change how a senior CV reads.

Treat a program as interview preparation. Write down what you built, which decisions you made, what broke and how you fixed it. In an interview you talk about that work, the same way you'd talk about projects from a job. I wrote more about this in the [guide on getting hired as an experienced developer](/academy/guides/getting-hired-with-ai/).

## Which Programs in Germany Start Above Beginner Level

In November 2026 we went through the AI course descriptions in the Bundesagentur für Arbeit catalogue. Most long programs started either from programming basics or from classical machine learning. Programs on LLM products or coding agents for people who already write code are rare, and many of them are short modules.

## FAQ

### Is a Coding Bootcamp Worth It for a Senior Developer?

Usually not. Most coding bootcamps teach programming from scratch, and a senior developer already programs at work.

### What Should a Senior Developer Look for in an AI Program?

A group where everyone already ships code, a syllabus about LLM products or coding agents, projects you can show in an interview, and a teacher who does this work.

### Can a Senior Developer Learn the Same AI Material for Free?

Yes. The DataTalks.Club Zoomcamps are free and cover the basics. A full-time program adds a fixed schedule, a small group and live teaching.
