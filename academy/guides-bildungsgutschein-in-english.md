## What a Bildungsgutschein Pays For

The Bildungsgutschein (education voucher) covers the course fees of further vocational training (berufliche Weiterbildung). According to the [Agentur für Arbeit](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein), the voucher is usually limited in time, region and educational goal, and you can only use it for a provider and a course that hold a Zulassung from an accredited body (fachkundige Stelle).

## Who Can Get One

The main route in [§ 81 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html) is for people who are unemployed, or whose unemployment is looming, when training is necessary to get them into work.

People who still have a job have a separate route in [§ 82 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__82.html), often called the Qualifizierungschancengesetz. The course has to last more than 120 hours and go beyond short, workplace-only training, the employer usually shares the cost, and the employer can apply. The [Agentur für Arbeit page on funding for employees](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/finanziell/foerderung-von-weiterbildung/individuelle-foerderung) has the current details.

The rules in § 81 SGB III do not mention citizenship. What counts is that you are registered with the Agentur für Arbeit in Germany and that the adviser finds the training necessary.

## Who Decides and On What Basis

Your adviser at the Agentur für Arbeit decides. Under § 81 Abs. 1 SGB III, training can be funded if three things are true:

1. the training is necessary to get you into work, or to prevent looming unemployment
2. the Agentur counselled you before the course starts
3. the provider and the course have an AZAV Zulassung

The key word is necessity (Notwendigkeit). The question is whether you can realistically find work without this specific course. Being unemployed is not enough on its own: there has to be a skills gap that the course closes, with a view to the jobs that are actually available.

The decision is discretionary (Ermessen). There is no automatic right to a voucher for further training, but you have a right to a decision that uses that discretion correctly.

## What the Law Says About People Who Already Have a Profession

Experienced developers often hear "you are qualified enough, you don't need training". [§ 81 Abs. 1a SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html) also recognizes necessity for unemployed people when added professional skills improve their individual employability and the training makes sense given the labour market.

In other words, years of experience are not the legal test by themselves. What matters is whether the skills that job postings now ask for are a gap for you, and whether the course closes it. Training like this, which adds skills to an existing profession, is called an Anpassungsqualifizierung. It is different from an Umschulung, which retrains you for a new profession.

## What AZAV Means and How to Check a Course

AZAV (Akkreditierungs- und Zulassungsverordnung Arbeitsförderung) is the regulation that sets the rules for training funded by the Agentur für Arbeit. There are two separate Zulassungen:

- the provider (Träger) needs a Trägerzulassung from a fachkundige Stelle
- each course (Maßnahme) needs its own Maßnahmezulassung

A course with a Maßnahmezulassung gets a Maßnahmenummer, the course number you see on its listing and on the voucher paperwork. You can search courses that can be funded with a voucher in [mein NOW](https://mein-now.de/), the Agentur für Arbeit's portal for further training, which replaced KURSNET as the public course search.

For developers, the necessity test usually comes down to one question: do the jobs you apply for ask for skills you don't have yet? ## Agentur für Arbeit or Jobcenter

Since 1 January 2025 the Agentur für Arbeit handles training advice and decides on the Bildungsgutschein, also if you receive Bürgergeld and first come through the Jobcenter. If the Jobcenter sees a need for training, it refers you to the Agentur, which does the counselling, checks the conditions and funds the course. The Jobcenter stays responsible for your Bürgergeld. The [Federal Ministry of Labour](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/das-aendert-sich-im-neuen-jahr.html) announced this change in December 2024.

## The Steps, From First Appointment to Course Start

This is how the process usually goes:

1. You talk to your adviser at the Agentur für Arbeit, or at the Jobcenter if you receive Bürgergeld.
2. In a counselling appointment, the adviser assesses whether training is necessary in your case. This has to happen before the course starts.
3. If the answer is yes, you receive the Bildungsgutschein for an educational goal, usually limited in time and region.
4. You choose a provider and a course with an AZAV Zulassung that matches the voucher, and hand in the voucher there.

Advisers often look for evidence of the gap: five to ten current job postings, ideally from your region, that ask for the skills you're missing, your CV with a short overview of the applications you have sent, and a short explanation of why this course closes the gap.

## German Level and Courses Taught in English

The rules in § 81 SGB III don't set a German level for the voucher. In practice, some advisers suggest learning German first. German is a good investment of your time. But if the jobs you apply for are in English, that is part of your case: the skills gap is what those jobs ask for.

## If the Answer Is No

An oral "no" in a meeting is not a formal decision. According to [Merkblatt 6](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-6-weiterbildung_ba015381.pdf), the Agentur notifies its decision in writing, also when it refuses. If you disagree with a written decision (Bescheid), you can file an objection (Widerspruch) within one month of notification, as set out in [§ 84 SGG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgg/__84.html) and in the Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung on the Bescheid.

## What Happens to Arbeitslosengeld During the Course

According to the [Agentur für Arbeit](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein), if you receive Arbeitslosengeld when you start a funded course, the benefit usually continues during the course. The exact details depend on your case, so ask your adviser.

## German Terms You Will See

- **Bildungsgutschein:** the education voucher that pays the course fees
- **Agentur für Arbeit:** the Federal Employment Agency, which decides on the voucher
- **Jobcenter:** the office responsible for Bürgergeld
- **Arbeitslosengeld (ALG I):** unemployment benefit from the Agentur für Arbeit
- **Bürgergeld:** basic income support, handled by the Jobcenter
- **Weiterbildungsberatung:** the counselling appointment about training
- **Notwendigkeit:** necessity, the main test for the voucher
- **Anpassungsqualifizierung:** training that adds skills to an existing profession
- **Umschulung:** retraining for a new profession
- **AZAV:** the regulation for training funded by the Agentur für Arbeit
- **Träger:** the training provider
- **Maßnahme:** the course
- **Zulassung:** the permission a provider or course needs for funding
- **fachkundige Stelle:** the accredited body that grants the Zulassung
- **Maßnahmenummer:** the course number of a course with a Zulassung
- **mein NOW:** the Agentur für Arbeit's portal for further training, which replaced KURSNET
- **Bescheid:** a formal written decision
- **Widerspruch:** an objection against a Bescheid

## FAQ

### Can Non-German Citizens Get a Bildungsgutschein?

The rules in § 81 SGB III do not mention citizenship. What counts is that you are registered with the Agentur für Arbeit in Germany and that the adviser finds the training necessary.

### Is There a Legal Right to a Bildungsgutschein?

No. For further training of people who already have a profession it is a discretionary decision. You have a right to a decision that uses that discretion correctly.
