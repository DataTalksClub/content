## What a Bildungsgutschein Pays For

The Bildungsgutschein (education voucher) pays the course fees for further vocational training (berufliche Weiterbildung). According to the [Agentur für Arbeit](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein), the voucher is usually limited in time, region and educational goal, and you can only use it with a provider and a course approved (Zulassung) by an accredited body (fachkundige Stelle).

## Who Can Get One

The main rule, [§ 81 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html), covers people who are unemployed or about to become unemployed, if they need training to find work.

If you still have a job, a separate rule applies: [§ 82 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__82.html), often called the Qualifizierungschancengesetz. The course has to last more than 120 hours and can't be just short training for your current workplace. The employer usually pays part of the cost and can also apply. You'll find the current details on the [Agentur für Arbeit page on funding for employees](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/finanziell/foerderung-von-weiterbildung/individuelle-foerderung).

The rules in § 81 SGB III don't mention citizenship. What counts is that you're registered with the Agentur für Arbeit in Germany and that your adviser thinks you need the training.

## Who Decides and On What Basis

Your adviser at the Agentur für Arbeit decides.

Under § 81 Abs. 1 SGB III, the Agentur can fund training if three things are true:

1. the training is necessary to get you into work, or to keep you from becoming unemployed
2. you've had a counselling appointment with the Agentur before the course starts
3. the provider and the course have an AZAV Zulassung

The key word is necessity (Notwendigkeit). Your adviser has to decide whether you could realistically find work without this particular course. Being unemployed isn't enough on its own. You need to be missing skills that the jobs on the market ask for, and the course has to teach them.

The adviser has discretion (Ermessen). You have no automatic right to a voucher, but you do have a right to a decision in which the adviser uses that discretion properly.

## What the Law Says About People Who Already Have a Profession

Experienced developers often hear "you're qualified enough, you don't need training". But under [§ 81 Abs. 1a SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html), training can also count as necessary for unemployed people if new professional skills make them easier to hire and the training makes sense for the labour market.

So having years of experience doesn't settle it. What matters is whether you're missing skills that job postings now ask for, and whether the course teaches them. Training like this, which adds skills to an existing profession, is called an Anpassungsqualifizierung. It's different from an Umschulung, which retrains you for a new profession.

## What AZAV Means and How to Check a Course

AZAV (Akkreditierungs- und Zulassungsverordnung Arbeitsförderung) is the regulation for training that the Agentur für Arbeit pays for.

There are two separate approvals (Zulassungen):

- the provider (Träger) needs a Trägerzulassung from a fachkundige Stelle
- each course (Maßnahme) needs its own Maßnahmezulassung

An approved course gets a Maßnahmenummer, the course number you see on its listing and on the voucher paperwork. To find courses you can pay for with a voucher, search [mein NOW](https://mein-now.de/), the Agentur für Arbeit's portal for further training. It replaced KURSNET as the public course search.

## Agentur für Arbeit or Jobcenter

Since 1 January 2025 the Agentur für Arbeit handles training counselling and decides on the Bildungsgutschein, even if you receive Bürgergeld and come through the Jobcenter first. If the Jobcenter thinks you need training, it sends you to the Agentur. The Agentur then gives you a counselling appointment, checks the conditions and pays for the course. Your Bürgergeld still comes from the Jobcenter. The [Federal Ministry of Labour](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2024/das-aendert-sich-im-neuen-jahr.html) announced this change in December 2024.

## The Steps, From First Appointment to Course Start

This is how it usually goes:

1. You talk to your adviser at the Agentur für Arbeit, or at the Jobcenter if you receive Bürgergeld.
2. In a counselling appointment, the adviser decides whether you need training. This has to happen before the course starts.
3. If the answer is yes, you get a Bildungsgutschein for a specific educational goal, usually limited in time and region.
4. You choose an approved provider and course (AZAV Zulassung) that match the voucher, and hand the voucher in to them.

Advisers often want to see proof that you're missing the skills the course teaches:

- five to ten current job postings, ideally from your region, that ask for the skills you're missing
- your CV with a short overview of the applications you've sent
- a short explanation of why this course is the right way to learn them

## German Level and Courses Taught in English

The rules in § 81 SGB III don't set a German level for the voucher. In practice, some advisers suggest learning German first, and that's worth your time. But if the jobs you apply for are in English, say so: the adviser compares your skills with what those jobs ask for.

## If the Answer Is No

A spoken "no" in a meeting isn't a formal decision. According to [Merkblatt 6](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/datei/merkblatt-6-weiterbildung_ba015381.pdf), the Agentur sends its decision in writing, even when it refuses. If you disagree with a written decision (Bescheid), you can file an objection (Widerspruch) within one month of receiving it, as set out in [§ 84 SGG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgg/__84.html) and in the Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung on the Bescheid.

## What Happens to Arbeitslosengeld During the Course

According to the [Agentur für Arbeit](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/bildungsgutschein), if you get Arbeitslosengeld when you start a funded course, you usually keep getting it during the course. The details depend on your case, so ask your adviser.

## German Terms You Will See

You'll see these words on forms and in letters from the Agentur:

- Bildungsgutschein: the education voucher that pays the course fees
- Agentur für Arbeit: the Federal Employment Agency, which decides on the voucher
- Jobcenter: the office responsible for Bürgergeld
- Arbeitslosengeld (ALG I): unemployment benefit from the Agentur für Arbeit
- Bürgergeld: basic income support, handled by the Jobcenter
- Weiterbildungsberatung: the counselling appointment about training
- Notwendigkeit: necessity, the main test for the voucher
- Anpassungsqualifizierung: training that adds skills to an existing profession
- Umschulung: retraining for a new profession
- AZAV: the regulation for training funded by the Agentur für Arbeit
- Träger: the training provider
- Maßnahme: the course
- Zulassung: the permission a provider or course needs for funding
- fachkundige Stelle: the accredited body that grants the Zulassung
- Maßnahmenummer: the course number of a course with a Zulassung
- mein NOW: the Agentur für Arbeit's portal for further training, which replaced KURSNET
- Bescheid: a formal written decision
- Widerspruch: an objection to a Bescheid

## FAQ

### Can Non-German Citizens Get a Bildungsgutschein?

The rules in § 81 SGB III don't mention citizenship. What counts is that you're registered with the Agentur für Arbeit in Germany and that your adviser thinks you need the training.

### Is There a Legal Right to a Bildungsgutschein?

No. If you already have a profession, further training is at the adviser's discretion. You do have a right to a decision in which the adviser uses that discretion properly.
