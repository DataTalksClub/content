Both carry the DataTalks.Club name, and the Academy is built on Zoomcamp material, but they are made for different people. This guide shows which one fits you.

Read about the [Agentic Engineering program](/academy/agentic-engineering/) and the [AI Engineering program](/academy/ai-engineering/), or **Join the Waitlist** for [Agentic Engineering](/academy/agentic-engineering/?utm_source=article&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=zoomcamps-vs-academy&utm_content=top#signup-hero) or [AI Engineering](/academy/ai-engineering/?utm_source=article&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=zoomcamps-vs-academy&utm_content=top#signup-hero).

## What the Zoomcamps Are

The Zoomcamps are free online courses, open to everyone. There is no application and no fee. Each one is built around pre-recorded modules, live office hours, homework with a leaderboard, and a final project that other learners review.

LLM Zoomcamp, for example, runs as a cohort once a year for about ten weeks, and its materials stay online for self-paced study between cohorts. You study around your job, at whatever pace you can keep.

The current list is on the [courses page](/courses). The two closest to the Academy are [LLM Zoomcamp](/courses/llm-zoomcamp) and [AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp](/courses/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp).

## What the Academy Is

[DataTalks.Club Academy](/academy/) is a full-time live program in English for experienced developers in Germany. It is for developers with 2+ years of experience.

- **Start:** April 2027
- **Length:** 3 months (13 weeks)
- **Schedule:** Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30 Berlin time
- **Where:** Berlin or live online
- **Language:** English
- **Group:** 15 seats
- **Funding:** Bildungsgutschein (coming soon).

All sessions are taught live, with no pre-recorded lectures. Online participants join live on the same Monday to Friday schedule.

## The Two Academy Programs and What Each Is Based On

The Academy has two programs.

**[Agentic Engineering](/academy/agentic-engineering/).** You learn to use coding agents like Claude Code or Cursor to write most of the code, while you decide what to build and check the result. The program is based on AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp and Alexey Grigorev's experience running coding agents 24/7 for the last year.

**[AI Engineering](/academy/ai-engineering/).** You learn to build and evaluate products that use LLMs, like chatbots, search over company documents and agents. The program is based on LLM Zoomcamp, AI Engineering Buildcamp, and Alexey's experience building AI products and putting them in production.

So the Zoomcamps are not replaced by the Academy. Each Academy program takes one Zoomcamp as its starting point and goes further, full-time and live.

## Side by Side

- **Cost.** Free Zoomcamps: Free. DataTalks.Club Academy: Paid. Bildungsgutschein (coming soon).
- **Schedule.** Free Zoomcamps: Pre-recorded modules and live office hours, at your own pace. DataTalks.Club Academy: Full-time, live, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30 Berlin time, 3 months.
- **Who it is for.** Free Zoomcamps: Anyone who wants to learn, anywhere. DataTalks.Club Academy: Developers in Germany with 2+ years of experience.
- **Group size.** Free Zoomcamps: Open to everyone. DataTalks.Club Academy: 15 seats.
- **Who teaches.** Free Zoomcamps: Course instructors, with help from the community in Slack. DataTalks.Club Academy: Alexey Grigorev, live, every session.
- **How to join.** Free Zoomcamps: Register for free on the course page. DataTalks.Club Academy: Join the waitlist, then apply when applications open.

## Who Should Stay With the Free Zoomcamps

The Zoomcamps fit you better if one of these is true:

- You have less than 2 years of experience as a developer, or you have never programmed. Start with the Zoomcamps and come back later.
- You want to keep your job and study in the evenings or at weekends. The Academy needs your full working day.
- You live outside Germany and want to learn at your own pace.
- You want to try a topic before you commit 3 months to it.

The Zoomcamps are also good preparation for the Academy, but you don't need to finish one to apply.

## Who the Academy Is For

The Academy is for developers with 2+ years of experience who live in Germany and can study full days, Monday to Friday, for 3 months. You may be laid off, in a notice period, job hunting, or employed and upskilling.

The programs don't start with basics like installing Git or Docker. They assume you already write code and want to learn agentic engineering or AI engineering in depth, with a teacher in the room or on the call every day.

## FAQ

### Is DataTalks.Club Academy Free Like the Zoomcamps?

No. The Zoomcamps are free online courses. DataTalks.Club Academy is a paid full-time live program. Bildungsgutschein funding is coming soon.

### Do I Need to Finish a Zoomcamp Before I Join DataTalks.Club Academy?

No. The Academy is for developers with 2+ years of experience. The free Zoomcamps are a good start if you have less experience.
