The Zoomcamps and the Academy both come from DataTalks.Club, and the Academy builds on Zoomcamp material, but they're for different people.

Read about the [Agentic Engineering program](/academy/agentic-engineering/) and the [AI Engineering program](/academy/ai-engineering/), or join the waitlist for [Agentic Engineering](/academy/agentic-engineering/?utm_source=article&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=zoomcamps-vs-academy&utm_content=top#signup-hero) or [AI Engineering](/academy/ai-engineering/?utm_source=article&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=zoomcamps-vs-academy&utm_content=top#signup-hero).

## What the Zoomcamps Are

The Zoomcamps are free online courses, open to everyone. There's no application and no fee. Each one has recorded modules, live office hours, homework with a leaderboard, and a final project that other learners review.

LLM Zoomcamp, for example, runs as a cohort once a year for about ten weeks, and the materials stay online so you can study at your own pace between cohorts. You fit it around your job.

You'll find the current list on the [courses page](/courses). The two closest to the Academy are [LLM Zoomcamp](/courses/llm-zoomcamp) and [AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp](/courses/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp).

## What the Academy Is

[DataTalks.Club Academy](/academy/) is a full-time live program in English for developers in Germany with 2+ years of experience.

- Start: April 2027
- Length: 3 months (13 weeks)
- Schedule: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30 Berlin time
- Where: Berlin or live online
- Language: English
- Group: 15 seats
- Funding: Bildungsgutschein (coming soon)

All sessions are live, and there are no recorded lectures. Online participants join live on the same Monday to Friday schedule.

## The Two Academy Programs and What Each Is Based On

The Academy has two programs.

In [Agentic Engineering](/academy/agentic-engineering/), you learn to use coding agents like Claude Code or Cursor to write most of the code, while you decide what to build and check the result. It's based on AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp and on Alexey Grigorev's experience running coding agents 24/7 for the last year.

In [AI Engineering](/academy/ai-engineering/), you learn to build and evaluate products that use LLMs, like chatbots, search over company documents and agents. It's based on LLM Zoomcamp, AI Engineering Buildcamp, and Alexey's experience building AI products and putting them into production.

So the Academy doesn't replace the Zoomcamps. Each Academy program starts from one Zoomcamp and goes further, full-time and live.

## Side by Side

- Cost: the Zoomcamps are free. DataTalks.Club Academy is paid, and Bildungsgutschein funding is coming soon.
- Schedule: the Zoomcamps have recorded modules and live office hours, at your own pace. The Academy is full-time and live, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30 Berlin time, for 3 months.
- Who it's for: the Zoomcamps are for anyone who wants to learn, anywhere. The Academy is for developers in Germany with 2+ years of experience.
- Group size: the Zoomcamps have no limit, since anyone can join. The Academy has 15 seats.
- Who teaches: course instructors teach the Zoomcamps, with help from the community in Slack. At the Academy, Alexey Grigorev teaches live, every session.
- How to join: for a Zoomcamp, register for free on the course page. For the Academy, join the waitlist, then apply when applications open.

## Who Should Stay With the Free Zoomcamps

The Zoomcamps are a better fit if:

- You have less than 2 years of experience as a developer, or you've never programmed. Start with the Zoomcamps and come back later.
- You want to keep your job and study in the evenings or at weekends. The Academy takes your whole working day.
- You live outside Germany and want to learn at your own pace.
- You want to try a topic before you spend 3 months on it.

A Zoomcamp is also good preparation for the Academy, but you don't need to finish one to apply.

## Who the Academy Is For

The Academy is for developers with 2+ years of experience who live in Germany and can study full days, Monday to Friday, for 3 months. You might have been laid off, be in a notice period, be looking for a job, or have a job and want to learn new skills.

Neither program starts with basics like installing Git or Docker. You should already write code and want to learn agentic engineering or AI engineering in depth, with a teacher in the room or on the call every day.

## Who Teaches

Alexey Grigorev teaches the Academy. He has taught more than 100,000 students in DataTalks.Club courses. He founded DataTalks.Club and created its free Zoomcamps. At the Academy he teaches every session himself and reviews every participant's project every week.

If you want to see how he teaches before you decide, join one of the [free Hireable With AI sessions](/academy/sessions/), short online talks with Q&A. They're separate events, not part of the Academy program.

## How to Join the Waitlist

1. Choose a program: [Agentic Engineering](/academy/agentic-engineering/) or [AI Engineering](/academy/ai-engineering/).
2. Leave your name and email on the program page. There's no commitment.
3. When applications open, people on the waitlist hear about it first.
4. Then you do a short practical task and have a 20-minute call, so you and Alexey can both check that the level is right for you.

## FAQ

### Is DataTalks.Club Academy Free Like the Zoomcamps?

No. The Zoomcamps are free online courses. DataTalks.Club Academy is a paid full-time live program. Bildungsgutschein funding is coming soon.

### Do I Need to Finish a Zoomcamp Before I Join DataTalks.Club Academy?

No. The Academy is for developers with 2+ years of experience. If you have less experience, the free Zoomcamps are a good place to start.

### When Does DataTalks.Club Academy Start?

It starts in April 2027, with 15 seats. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first when applications open.
