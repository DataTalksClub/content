## What "AI Bootcamp" Means in Germany

The words "AI bootcamp" cover three different products in Germany:

1. **Long programs for career starters.** Several months full-time, often starting from programming basics, built for people who want to enter tech.
2. **Short courses without code.** A few days on what AI can do for a business. Some of the courses that come up first when you search for "AI bootcamp" in Germany are of this kind, like a 5-day course with no coding or live workshops for people in finance.
3. **Programs for working developers.** They assume you can already program and spend the time on building with LLMs and coding agents. There are only a few.

If you already work as a developer, the first thing to check in any list is the entry level. Length, language and format come after that.

## AI Bootcamps for Career Starters

**Ironhack** says of its AI Engineering bootcamp that no prior technical experience is needed. You do 40+ hours of prework, then 9 weeks full-time or 24 weeks part-time, live online in English.

**Le Wagon** says no technical experience or prior programming knowledge is needed for its AI Software bootcamp in Berlin, after a 40-hour prep course. It runs 2 months full-time or 6 months part-time, in person in Berlin or online.

**WBS Coding School** says most of its courses are designed for beginners and career changers. Its AI Software Development course runs 17 weeks full-time, Monday to Friday, online, in English or German. It also lists programs of 10 or 12 months.

These programs are a good fit if you are new to tech. If you have years of experience, you would spend much of the time on things you already do at work.

## AI Bootcamps for Developers Who Already Code

A few programs ask for programming experience before you start:

- **Spiced Academy** asks for programming skills, ideally in Python, and for comfort with numbers, algebra and statistics in its AI Engineering program. It runs 32 weeks full-time, in Berlin or online. **neue fische** lists the same 32-week AI Engineering program. Its page names basic programming in the requirements, but also says no IT background is needed.
- **Le Wagon's Data Science & AI** bootcamp requires basic programming and maths and runs 2 months full-time.
- **Digitale Leute School** says that ideally you bring first practical experience in software development, and that you don't need to be a senior engineer. Its full-time format is aimed at newcomers and career changers, its part-time format at people who study alongside their job.
- **Turing College** asks for at least a year of Python or JavaScript for its AI Engineering program, and says it is for developers who want to use their coding skills to build LLM applications and agents. It is self-paced, with no live lectures, over 3 to 4 months.

So "for people who already code" usually means a year of experience or basic programming, not several years of professional work.

## AI Courses for Experienced Developers in Germany

If you have several years of experience and want to upskill in AI, the options fall into three groups by length:

- **Short workshops of 2 to 5 days.** Good for an overview or one tool. Too short to build and evaluate a real project.
- **Programs of about 3 months.** Digitale Leute School and Turing College sit here, and so does DataTalks.Club Academy. Check whether the format is live or self-paced, and what experience the rest of the group brings.
- **Long programs of 8 months or more.** Spiced Academy and neue fische run 32 weeks for AI Engineering, and WBS Coding School lists programs of 10 or 12 months. They cover a lot of ground, and they take a large part of a year.

## Short Courses and Workshops

Short courses are a different product. The 5-day AI bootcamp at artificialintelligencebootcamp.de says it involves no coding and is open to anyone, in Frankfurt or as a live webinar. zeb.business school runs AI bootcamp workshops for staff of financial institutions.

These help managers and business teams understand AI. They don't teach a developer to build, test and ship an LLM application.

## How to Choose by Experience Level

- **Under 2 years as a developer.** A career-starter program or a free course fits. The free [DataTalks.Club Zoomcamps](/courses) are a good start, for example [LLM Zoomcamp](/courses/llm-zoomcamp).
- **2+ years as a developer.** Look for a program that skips the basics and spends its time on building with LLMs or coding agents. Ask who else is in the group.
- **Team leads.** You may not need a full program. A short workshop for the overview, plus time to build one project yourself, can be enough. If you want to build hands-on again, a 3-month program fits.

## Questions to Ask Any Bootcamp Before You Sign

- **What is the entry level of the group?** Ask what most participants did before the program.
- **Who teaches?** One instructor every day, or rotating teachers and mentors?
- **How big is the group?**
- **What do you build?** Ask to see projects from earlier participants.
- **Is it live or self-paced?** Recorded videos with office hours are a different product from daily live teaching.
