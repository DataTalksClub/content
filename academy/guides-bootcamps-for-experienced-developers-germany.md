## What to Compare If You Already Work as a Developer

Most bootcamp comparisons are written for career changers and compare price and city. If you already work as a developer, ask different questions:

- **Entry level of the group.** Does the program start from programming basics, or does it assume you already code?
- **What you would repeat.** Git, HTML, a first web app, SQL basics: if the first weeks cover what you do at work, that is time you pay for twice.
- **What is new.** Building with LLMs, evaluation, coding agents, deployment of AI products.
- **Who teaches.** One instructor, rotating teachers, or mentors on top of recorded material.
- **Group size.** It decides how much attention your project gets.

## Le Wagon for Experienced Developers

Le Wagon teaches in Berlin, in person or online, full-time or part-time. Its Berlin pages list AI Software, Data Science & AI and Data Analytics. Full-time runs 2 months and part-time runs 6 months. Its FAQ says the bootcamps are taught mainly in English in most cities.

On entry level, Le Wagon says no technical experience or prior programming knowledge is needed for AI Software, after a 40-hour prep course. Data Analytics is described as beginner-friendly. Data Science & AI asks for basic programming and maths.

**For an experienced developer:** AI Software starts where a career changer starts, so you would likely repeat much of it. Data Science & AI starts higher, with basic programming and maths as the entry level. Le Wagon is a strong option if you want to learn in a Berlin classroom and are new to the field.

## Ironhack for Experienced Developers

Ironhack's Berlin pages list AI Engineering, Web Development, Data Analytics, UX/UI Design and others. The cohorts on those pages are all listed as remote, live online, in English. Full-time runs 9 weeks for AI Engineering and 11 weeks for Web Development, and part-time runs 24 weeks.

For AI Engineering, Ironhack says no prior technical experience is needed, with 40+ hours of prework before the start.

**For an experienced developer:** the AI Engineering bootcamp is open to people with no technical background, so you would likely repeat much of the early material. Ironhack fits if you want a short program with a part-time option and are new to programming.

## Le Wagon vs Ironhack: What Differs for Someone Who Already Codes

- **Classroom.** Le Wagon offers in-person classes in Berlin. Ironhack's Berlin cohorts are listed as live online.
- **Length.** Le Wagon full-time runs 2 months. Ironhack full-time runs 9 to 11 weeks, depending on the bootcamp.
- **Part-time.** Both have it: 6 months at Le Wagon, 24 weeks at Ironhack.
- **Entry level.** Both say their main bootcamps need no prior programming. Le Wagon's Data Science & AI is the exception, with basic programming and maths.
- **Language.** Both teach in English.

For someone who already codes, the two are closer than they look. Both are built for career changers, so the choice between them comes down to classroom or online and the schedule. Ironhack has published [its own comparison of the two](https://www.ironhack.com/us/blog/choosing-the-right-tech-bootcamp-ironhack-vs-le-wagon), written from Ironhack's side.

## Spiced Academy for Experienced Developers

Spiced Academy lists more than twenty programs, many of them with AI in the name. The entry level depends on the program:

- **Software Development with AI:** 16 weeks full-time, remote. Spiced says it is designed for everyone, regardless of previous knowledge.
- **Data Science & AI:** 16 weeks full-time, in Berlin or online, for people with some prior knowledge of programming or maths and statistics.
- **AI Engineering:** 32 weeks full-time, in Berlin or online. It asks for programming skills, ideally in Python, and for comfort with numbers, algebra and statistics.

AI Engineering has English cohorts and a German remote cohort, and Software Development with AI is taught in German. neue fische lists the same AI Engineering program, also 32 weeks.

**For an experienced developer:** AI Engineering asks for the most before you start, and Data Science & AI also expects some programming or maths. At 32 weeks full-time, AI Engineering is long. It fits if you want a long, broad program and can study full-time for 8 months.

## WBS Coding School for Experienced Developers

WBS Coding School teaches online, in English or German, and asks for B2 in the language of instruction. Its bootcamps include AI Software Development (17 weeks), Data Science (17 weeks), Data Analytics (13 weeks) and AI Agents & Automations (12 weeks), all full-time. It also lists programs of 10 or 12 months.

WBS Coding School says most of its courses are designed for beginners and career changers. AI Software Development runs Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30.

**For an experienced developer:** the courses are built for people starting out, so you would likely repeat the early modules. WBS Coding School fits if you are changing careers and want to study online in German or English.

## What Reviews Say and What They Leave Out

Review sites like [Course Report's Berlin page](https://www.coursereport.com/cities/berlin) collect reviews from bootcamp graduates, and Reddit threads compare the schools too. These schools are built mainly for career changers, so most reviews tell you how a program felt to someone new to tech.

What they rarely say is how the program felt to someone who already worked as a developer. When you read reviews, look for reviewers with a background like yours, and check what they said about the first weeks.

## FAQ

Le Wagon's AI Software and Data Analytics bootcamps need no prior programming, and Data Science & AI asks for basic programming and maths. Full-time runs 2 months. A developer with several years of experience would likely repeat much of the material.

### Which Bootcamps in Germany Are Built for People Who Already Code?

Turing College asks for at least a year of Python or JavaScript, and Digitale Leute School prefers first practical experience in software development. DataTalks.Club Academy is for developers with 2+ years of experience.

### Is DataTalks.Club Academy a Coding Bootcamp?

No. It does not teach programming from the start. It is a 3-month full-time live program in English with two programs, Agentic Engineering and AI Engineering, for developers with 2+ years of experience. Start: April 2027.
