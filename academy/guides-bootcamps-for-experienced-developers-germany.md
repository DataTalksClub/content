## What to Compare If You Already Work as a Developer

Most bootcamp comparisons are written for career changers, and they compare price and city.

If you already work as a developer, you need to ask other questions:

- Who's in the group: does the program start from programming basics, or do you need to know how to code already?
- What you would repeat: Git, HTML, a first web app, SQL basics. If the first weeks cover what you do at work anyway, you pay to learn things you already know.
- What's new for you: building with LLMs, evaluation, coding agents, deploying AI products.
- Who teaches: one instructor, rotating teachers, or mentors on top of recorded videos.
- How big the group is: the smaller the group, the more attention your project gets.

## Le Wagon for Experienced Developers

Le Wagon teaches in Berlin, in person or online, full-time or part-time. On its Berlin pages it lists AI Software, Data Science & AI and Data Analytics. The full-time bootcamps take 2 months and the part-time ones take 6 months. According to its FAQ, in most cities the bootcamps are taught mainly in English.

According to Le Wagon, you don't need technical experience or programming knowledge for AI Software, as long as you do a 40-hour prep course first. It calls Data Analytics beginner-friendly. For Data Science & AI, you need basic programming and maths.

AI Software starts at the career-changer level, so as an experienced developer you'd probably repeat much of it. Data Science & AI starts at a higher level, because you need basic programming and maths to get in. Le Wagon is a strong option if you're new to the field and want to learn in a classroom in Berlin.

## Ironhack for Experienced Developers

On its Berlin pages, Ironhack lists AI Engineering, Web Development, Data Analytics, UX/UI Design and others. All the cohorts on those pages are remote: live online, in English. Full-time takes 9 weeks for AI Engineering and 11 weeks for Web Development, and part-time takes 24 weeks.

Ironhack says you don't need any technical experience for AI Engineering, but you do 40+ hours of prework before the start.

Since the AI Engineering bootcamp accepts people with no technical background, as an experienced developer you'd probably repeat much of the early material. Ironhack is a good fit if you're new to programming and want a short program with a part-time option.

## Le Wagon vs Ironhack: What Differs for Someone Who Already Codes

- Classroom: Le Wagon has in-person classes in Berlin. Ironhack's Berlin cohorts are live online.
- Length: full-time takes 2 months at Le Wagon and 9 to 11 weeks at Ironhack, depending on the bootcamp.
- Part-time: both have it, 6 months at Le Wagon and 24 weeks at Ironhack.
- Entry level: both say you don't need to know how to program for their main bootcamps. The exception is Le Wagon's Data Science & AI, where you need basic programming and maths.
- Language: both teach in English.

If you already code, the two are more alike than they look. Both are built for career changers, so you'd choose based on whether you want to learn in a classroom or online, and which schedule suits you. Ironhack has published [its own comparison of the two](https://www.ironhack.com/us/blog/choosing-the-right-tech-bootcamp-ironhack-vs-le-wagon), written from its side.

## Spiced Academy for Experienced Developers

Spiced Academy lists more than twenty programs, and many of them have AI in the name.

What you need to get in depends on the program:

- Software Development with AI: 16 weeks full-time, remote. Spiced says it's designed for everyone, whatever you already know.
- Data Science & AI: 16 weeks full-time, in Berlin or online, for people who already know some programming or maths and statistics.
- AI Engineering: 32 weeks full-time, in Berlin or online. You need programming skills, ideally in Python, and you should be comfortable with numbers, algebra and statistics.

AI Engineering has English cohorts and a German remote cohort, and Software Development with AI is taught in German. neue fische lists the same AI Engineering program, which also takes 32 weeks.

Of the three, AI Engineering has the highest entry requirements: programming, ideally Python, plus algebra and statistics. For Data Science & AI you also need some programming or maths. AI Engineering is long, though: 32 weeks full-time. It fits if you want a broad program and can study full-time for 8 months.

## WBS Coding School for Experienced Developers

WBS Coding School teaches online, in English or German, and you need B2 in the language you study in. Its bootcamps include AI Software Development (17 weeks), Data Science (17 weeks), Data Analytics (13 weeks) and AI Agents & Automations (12 weeks), all full-time. It also lists programs of 10 or 12 months.

WBS Coding School says most of its courses are designed for beginners and career changers. AI Software Development runs Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30.

The courses are built for people who are starting out, so as an experienced developer you'd probably repeat the early modules. WBS Coding School is a good fit if you're changing careers and want to study online in German or English.

## What Reviews Say and What They Leave Out

Review sites like [Course Report's Berlin page](https://www.coursereport.com/cities/berlin) collect reviews from bootcamp graduates, and people compare the schools on Reddit too. Most students at these schools are career changers, so most reviews tell you what a program was like for someone new to tech.

Reviews rarely tell you what it was like for someone who already worked as a developer. When you read them, look for reviewers with a background like yours, and check what they said about the first weeks.

## Alternatives for Developers Who Already Code

If you already code, you have a few other options:

- Turing College wants at least a year of Python or JavaScript for its AI Engineering program. It's self-paced and online, and takes 3 to 4 months.
- For AI Software Engineering, Digitale Leute School says that ideally you already have some practical experience in software development. It takes 3 months, online, and you can do it full-time, part-time or as a video course.
- Short workshops of 2 to 5 days give you an overview of one topic or tool, but they're too short to build a real project.

## FAQ

### Is Le Wagon Suitable for Experienced Developers?

You don't need to know how to program for Le Wagon's AI Software and Data Analytics bootcamps, and for Data Science & AI you need basic programming and maths. Full-time takes 2 months. A developer with several years of experience would probably repeat much of the material.

### Which Bootcamps in Germany Are Built for People Who Already Code?

Turing College wants at least a year of Python or JavaScript, and Digitale Leute School prefers people with some practical experience in software development. DataTalks.Club Academy is for developers with 2+ years of experience.

### Is DataTalks.Club Academy a Coding Bootcamp?

No. It doesn't teach programming from scratch. It's a 3-month full-time live program in English for developers with 2+ years of experience. There are two programs: Agentic Engineering and AI Engineering. It starts in April 2027.
