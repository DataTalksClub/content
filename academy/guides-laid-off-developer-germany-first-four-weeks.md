Rules and pages were checked on 24 November 2026. For your own case, the Agentur für Arbeit is the place to ask.

## Week 1: The Two Registrations

There are two separate registrations, and you need both. The Agentur für Arbeit says it plainly: the jobseeker registration does not replace the unemployment registration.

**1. Register as a jobseeker (arbeitsuchend melden).** You have to do this at the latest three months before your job ends. If you learn about the end date less than three months before it, you have three days from the day you learn it. This is § 38 Abs. 1 SGB III, and it applies even if you are fighting the dismissal in court or your employer hints that you might stay ([§ 38 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__38.html)).

You can register online, by phone at the free service number 0800 4 555500, or in person. The Agentur recommends online as the easiest way, and asks you to use only one of them ([Wie Sie sich arbeitsuchend melden](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitsuchend-melden)).

**2. Register as unemployed (arbeitslos melden).** You can do this at the earliest three months before you become unemployed, and at the latest on your first day without work. Arbeitslosengeld is paid at the earliest from the day of this registration, so a late registration means days without money ([Wie Sie sich arbeitslos melden](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitslos-melden)).

You can register as unemployed online or in person ([§ 141 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__141.html)). Online, you identify yourself with the online function of your ID card, your electronic residence permit or an eID card, and you need the PIN for it. In person, bring your ID card with your current address, or a passport with a current Meldebescheinigung.

**What a late jobseeker registration costs.** If you register as a jobseeker too late, the Agentur imposes a one-week Sperrzeit, a week in which no Arbeitslosengeld is paid ([§ 159 Abs. 6 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__159.html), and the Bundesagentur's [press release of 3 September 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/en/press/2026-33-register-as-a-jobseeker-in-good-time-make-the-most-of-opportunities-and-avoid-a-waiting-period)).

## Week 1: Before You Sign Anything

Many layoffs come with an offer: leave by agreement, often with a severance payment. In German this is an Aufhebungsvertrag, and the payment is an Abfindung.

Be careful here. The Bundesagentur lists an Aufhebungsvertrag, for example one with a severance payment, among the reasons for a Sperrzeit, and so does your own resignation. A Sperrzeit after giving up a job can last up to 12 weeks without Arbeitslosengeld. An important reason, with proof, can prevent it ([Arbeitslosengeld: Anspruch, Höhe, Dauer](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer), [§ 159 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__159.html)).

Before you sign, ask the Agentur für Arbeit or an employment lawyer what the agreement means for your Arbeitslosengeld. A few days of waiting cost less than three months without benefit.

## Week 2: Arbeitslosengeld in Short

You can get Arbeitslosengeld when all of this is true ([Arbeitslosengeld: Anspruch, Höhe, Dauer](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer)):

- you have no job, but you can work at least 15 hours a week in a job with social insurance
- you registered as unemployed
- you are looking for a job and work with the Agentur für Arbeit on it
- you were insured for at least 12 months in the 30 months before (the Anwartschaftszeit in [§ 142 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__142.html), within the Rahmenfrist of 30 months in [§ 143 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__143.html))

The amount is 60 percent of a calculated net daily wage, or 67 percent if you or your spouse or partner have at least one child. The calculation starts from your gross salary over the last 12 months. Under 50, you get it for at most 12 months, if you were insured for at least 24 months (same [Bundesagentur page](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/finanzielle-hilfen/arbeitslosengeld-anspruch-hoehe-dauer)).

Registering as unemployed is not the same as applying. You submit the application for Arbeitslosengeld separately, and you can do it online.

## Week 2: If You Are on a Residence Permit Tied to Your Job

If your residence permit depends on your job, for example an EU Blue Card, losing the job affects your permit too. The rules depend on the type of permit and how long you have held it, so do not guess. Contact your Ausländerbehörde early and read the official [questions and answers about the EU Blue Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/pdf-questions-answers-eu-blue-card) on Make it in Germany, the federal government's portal for skilled workers.

## Week 3: Your Documents and Your Story

Once the paperwork is done, prepare what you will need for every application:

- **Your CV.** Update it while you still remember the details of your last projects.
- **Your reference letter (Arbeitszeugnis).** In Germany you have the right to a written reference when you leave ([§ 109 GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__109.html)). Ask for it early, and read it before you accept it.
- **What you shipped.** Write down the systems you built, the problems you solved and the numbers you can share. This is the material for interviews, and it is easiest to write now.

Then write your short story: why you are looking, what you do best, and what you want to do next. You will tell it in every first call.

## Week 4: How to Use the Gap

A gap on a CV is not a problem when you can say what you did with it. You can use it in several ways, and you can combine them:

- **Job search.** Networking and referrals work better than mass applications. I wrote about this in the [guide on getting hired as an experienced developer](/academy/guides/getting-hired-with-ai/).
- **Free courses** in your own time, while you keep looking for work.
- **Short workshops** for one specific skill.
- **A full-time program** of about three months, if you want to change how you work.

Many developer job ads now ask for AI skills. In our count of developer jobs in Germany on arbeitnow in the week of 7 to 14 November 2026, 86 of 588 ads named Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Codex or AI-assisted coding. This is an approximate count we made by searching the ad texts for these terms.

## Training While You Are Unemployed: What Exists

The Bildungsgutschein (education voucher) exists to fund further training through the Agentur für Arbeit. There is no general right to it: the Agentur decides case by case whether training is needed for you to find work again ([§ 81 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__81.html), [Förderung der beruflichen Weiterbildung](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/karriere-und-weiterbildung/foerderung-berufliche-weiterbildung)). ## German Terms You Will See

- **arbeitsuchend:** looking for work. You register as arbeitsuchend while you still have a job that is ending.
- **arbeitslos:** unemployed. You register as arbeitslos at the latest on your first day without work.
- **Arbeitslosengeld (ALG I):** unemployment benefit, paid from the unemployment insurance.
- **Sperrzeit:** a period without Arbeitslosengeld, for example after a late jobseeker registration or after giving up a job.
- **Aufhebungsvertrag:** an agreement with your employer to end the job, often with a severance payment (Abfindung).
- **Agentur für Arbeit:** the local employment agency of the Bundesagentur für Arbeit. It handles registration and Arbeitslosengeld.
- **Jobcenter:** the office for basic income support, for people who do not get Arbeitslosengeld or whose Arbeitslosengeld is not enough to live on.

## FAQ

### When Do I Have to Register as a Jobseeker After a Layoff in Germany?

At the latest three months before your job ends. If you learn the end date less than three months before it, within three days of learning it.

### Is Registering as a Jobseeker the Same as Registering as Unemployed?

No. They are two steps. You register as unemployed at the latest on your first day without a job, and Arbeitslosengeld is paid at the earliest from that registration.

### Can I Study While I Am Unemployed in Germany?

Free courses in your own time are always possible. For full-time training, talk to the Agentur für Arbeit first: to get Arbeitslosengeld you have to stay available for work, and training you arrange yourself keeps you available only if the Agentur agrees to it ([§ 139 Abs. 3 SGB III](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__139.html)).
